fix: overlapping floor bands no longer flip the patrol layer (flicker guard)

Observed in the live log right after the climb to layer3:
  LAYER CHANGED: layer3 -> layer2 at y=0.348958; restarting layer2 patrol
  MAP LOOP reset world Y at layer2=0.103357  (while the character visibly
  stands on layer3)

Adjacent floors' recorded Y bands can overlap (span +- tolerance), so a
Y-only reading hits BOTH floors; the resync then re-targeted the other
floor's patrol points mid-patrol, so the patrol never completed - the
character was stuck patrolling layer3 forever and the range-top drop to
layer2 never fired.

- _resync_route_layer: a marker-only ambiguous reading (no confident
  world-Y) that still falls inside the CURRENT layer's recorded band now
  keeps the current layer instead of switching/returning.  A confident
  world-Y structure read stays authoritative (centered/aliased marker
  disambiguation and fall recovery are preserved), and readings clearly
  outside the current band still switch/return.
- test_movement_worker: overlap-band keep-current coverage (both floors,
  ambiguous + unambiguous readings).

Works together with the range-top drop fix: the layer3 patrol now
finishes, the range top drops back to layer2, and the loop repeats.
